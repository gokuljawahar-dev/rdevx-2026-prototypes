Who this serves

Three connected governance personas.

Each persona has a distinct intent and the governance evidence the design must surface for them.

Engagement Manager · primary

Govern engagements proactively. Receives an integrated governance view with early-warning signals, progress health, exceptions and action ownership — one gap, one next operation.

Delivery Manager · Scrum Master

Keep plans execution-ready and expose delivery risk before it hits commitments. Sees backlog readiness, sprint health, blocker aging, capacity variance and action tracking.

Product Owner · Customer Stakeholder

Make informed prioritization, release and scope decisions on current evidence. Sees approved scope, change impact, milestone readiness, quality thresholds and decision logs.

Design language for Horizon

Purpose drives the shell, rhythm and interaction model.

Layout archetype

Dense executive command cockpit: exception-first attention, six-lever diagnosis, evidence drill-down and governed action.

Typography

Tighter executive system typography with compact headings, tabular figures and a controlled uppercase label layer.

Color & surface

Deep ink and midnight surfaces, turquoise evidence, muted gold authority, coral exception and gray-blue Unknown; elevation is expressed with rules, not floating cards.

Spacing & density

A disciplined 4/8/12/20px grid with compact tables and a user-controlled comfortable/compact density mode.

Signature component

Exception Priority Queue + six independent lever scorecards: one objective gap is visible without blending away hard gates.

Screen-space controls

Persistent sidebar collapse ([), focus mode (F), right evidence-panel toggle, density toggle (D) and remembered settings.
